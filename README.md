# Closet Pilot 🧥✈️

An AI-powered assistant and inventory manager designed to take the friction out of decluttering your wardrobe and selling unwanted clothes online.

## Core Features

1. **Sell, Consign, or Donate Advisor**
   - Upload photos of a garment (and its brand/care tags) to quickly determine if it is worth selling individually, sending to bulk consignment (e.g., ThredUp, Crossroads), or donating.
2. **AI Listing Photo Coach**
   - Evaluates your photos for lighting, framing, background clutter, and completeness (e.g., missing back view, brand tag, fabric content tag, or flaw close-up) with actionable tips to improve them.
3. **Platform & Pricing Intelligence**
   - Recommends the best platforms for each specific piece (Poshmark, Depop, eBay, Grailed, Mercari, The RealReal, Facebook Marketplace) and suggests competitive price ranges based on comparable sold listings.
4. **Smart Measurement Checklist**
   - Automatically identifies the exact measurements buyers expect for that garment category (e.g., pit-to-pit, shoulder, sleeve, and back length for jackets; waist, rise, inseam, and leg opening for pants) plus standard brand size chart context.
5. **Snap-to-Listing Generator**
   - Generates ready-to-copy titles, detailed descriptions, condition notes, and SEO tags tailored to each target marketplace.
6. **Closet Declutter & Inventory Tracker**
   - Tracks items across their lifecycle: `To Photograph` → `Ready to List` → `Listed` → `Sold` (or `Donate after 30 days`).
